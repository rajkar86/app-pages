Spelling Bee Unlimited: Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Email kraj.console+spellingbee@gmail.com. In the app you can also open Settings, then Give feedback. It opens a draft in your email app with your app version and device model filled in, so a problem is easier to reproduce. Nothing is sent until you press send.

How to play

  1. Seven letters are shown in a hexagon layout. One letter, in the center, is required.
  2. Tap letters to build a word of 4 or more letters that includes the center letter. Letters can be reused.
  3. Tap Check to submit. Tap Shuffle to rearrange the outer letters. Tap Clear to start the word again.
  4. Scoring: a 4-letter word is 1 point, a longer word is 1 point per letter, and a pangram (uses all seven letters) earns 7 extra points.
  5. Tap New Game in the menu for a fresh puzzle whenever you like.

Questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. The game works offline.

A real word was rejected, or an odd word was accepted. Please send it through Give feedback. The word lists are long and not filtered for content.

My game disappeared. Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app or resetting the device removes it.

Give feedback does nothing. It opens your email app. If no email account is set up, the app shows the support address so you can write from anywhere.
